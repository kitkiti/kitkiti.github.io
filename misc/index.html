outside research

Outside research and teaching, I enjoy staying active. I used to be an athlete while studying at a military boarding school for girls in Feni, Bangladesh. Over time, however, continuing some of those hobbies has become increasingly difficult as a middle-class woman living in Dhaka’s conservative, concrete-dense urban environment. I still miss being able to run, play, and spend hours outdoors.
