HyperCite is a collaborative research platform for organizing papers, asking grounded questions across a literature corpus, and tracing AI-generated claims back to their exact sources.

Team HyperCite was named **Champion** at the BRACU CSE471 System Analysis and Design Project Showcase for Fall 2025. The final project demonstration took place on 7 May 2026 after the course's 120+ projects had been evaluated.

## Highlights

- Built a hybrid retrieval-augmented generation pipeline combining MongoDB Atlas vector search with lexical fallback.
- Diversified retrieved chunks across papers to ground answers in a broader literature corpus.
- Anchored citations to PDF excerpts using page-level character offsets, allowing users to navigate from a generated claim to its source.
- Added real-time collaborative annotation through Liveblocks.

## Technologies

React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Supabase, and Liveblocks.
