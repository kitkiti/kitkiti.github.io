I am an Adjunct Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University.

In Summer 2026, I taught four undergraduate laboratory sections: CSE110: Programming Language I, CSE321: Operating Systems, and two sections of CSE330: Numerical Methods. I also held three hours of weekly consultation sessions supporting students with laboratory coursework, programming concepts, and problem solving.

Previously, from November 2024 to May 2025, I worked as a Student Tutor, providing laboratory and one-to-one academic support for first- and second-year CSE courses, including CSE220: Data Structures.
